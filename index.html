This is a keeper league. Managers may carry players over from one season to the next, subject to the limits and costs below.

RuleDetail
Keepers allowedUp to 2 keepers per manager. A player can't be kept consecutive years. If any league manager kept a player the previous year then they cannot be kept under any circumstances
Keeper costOne draft pick per keeper, in the round the player was originally drafted
Keeper Value FloorA keeper costs no more than a 10th-round pick. This applies regardless of whether they were drafted or picked up off waivers. This keeps the overall talent distribution slightly more balanced and prevents overwhelming advantages
Same-round bumpIf a manager keeps two players from the same round, one of them bumps up a round
Keeper lock deadlineKeepers must be finalized 3 days before the draft. After the lock date, keeper selections are final
Same-round bump When two keepers land in the same draft round, the second keeper is bumped to one round earlier.
Keeper lock — no exceptions The keeper deadline is 3 days before the draft. Once the lock date passes, keeper selections are final — no changes of any kind are allowed, including for injuries.

Proposed rule changes are put to a vote on the day of the draft.

Adoption threshold 8 of 12 votes in favor are required to add or change a rule. Any rule change that passes takes effect the following season — it does not apply mid-season.
